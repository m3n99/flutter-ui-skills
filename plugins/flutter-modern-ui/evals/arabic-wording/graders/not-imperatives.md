PASS if the button labels are verbal nouns, the usual form in Arabic interfaces (حفظ، إلغاء، حذف، إضافة …، التالي), and the reply explains that Arabic needs more plural forms than English (zero, one, two, few, many, other) handled by the localization system's plural rules rather than by joining strings.

FAIL if the buttons are imperatives addressed to the user (احفظ، ألغِ، احذف، أضف) or the plural advice covers only singular and plural.
